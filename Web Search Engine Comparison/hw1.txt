In this analysis, I compared the search results from Bing against the Google reference dataset using two metrics: percentage overlap and Spearman's rank correlation coefficient. 
The results were calculated for 100 queries, and the averages for both metrics are provided below.

Average Percent Overlap: 9.20%
This indicates that, on average, approximately 9.2% of the URLs in the top 10 results from the assigned search engine overlap with those in Google's top 10 results for the same query. 
The low overlap suggests that the search engine returns significantly different results compared to Google for most queries.


Average Spearman Coefficient: 0.10
The average Spearman correlation coefficient between the rankings of the URLs returned by the two search engines is 0.10. 
This low positive correlation indicates that there is little similarity in the ranking of common URLs between the two search engines. 
In many cases, either no URLs were shared between the two search engines, or the ranking order of the matching URLs was not well-aligned.
